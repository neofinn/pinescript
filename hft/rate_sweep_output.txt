RATE CEILING  (exchange-member seat)
  Retail broker caps (10/s, 400/min, 5,000/day) no longer apply.
  NSE sets a message rate PER SESSION PER MEMBER; colocation LAN
  sessions get the configured rate +10%. That number is in your
  connectivity agreement -- this lab cannot look it up for you.

  Still live at member level:
    * order-to-trade ratio penalties fall on the MEMBER. SEBI's
      2026-02-04 revision exempts option orders within +/-40% of
      LTP or +/-Rs20, whichever is higher, so at-the-touch orders
      are outside the framework. A passive book that cancels most
      of what it quotes is the case to check, not this one.
    * every algo needs its exchange-approved unique identifier.

SWEEP D -- which PREMIUM band pays, now that cost scales with it.
  Break-even ticks = 0.2383% x premium / tick. A 500-rupee option
  must move 23.8 ticks to cover a round trip; a 50-rupee one, 2.4.
  The counter-force is that cheap options have proportionally the
  widest spreads, so this is an optimum, not a slope.
